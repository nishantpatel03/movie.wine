Frequently Asked Questions

How do I upgrade to the Director's Tier?

expand_more
You can upgrade your membership at any time from your Account Settings. The Director's Tier includes exclusive access to pre-release screenings and our private film critique forums.

Are the wine pairings included in the subscription?

expand_more
Digital pairing guides are included in all tiers. Physical wine deliveries are exclusively available to our "Sommelier & Cinema" package members in select regions.

Can I submit my own short films for review?

expand_more
Absolutely. We host a monthly "Indie Spotlight" where members can submit their work. Selected films receive professional critique from our panel of experts.
